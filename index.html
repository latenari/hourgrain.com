How the estimate works

Trust the trend, not the day.

Most trackers start your budget from a formula and never correct it. Hourgrain works energy balance backwards from what you log and how your weight trends, so the number it gives you is your own.

Promises

Paid for by the people who use it.

No one else pays for Hourgrain, so no one else gets your food log or your weight. That decides what it will and won't do.

blockNo ads, ever

Not on the free tier, not on Premium. Nothing about you is sold or shared for marketing.

shieldNo tracking kits

There are no ad or analytics SDKs in the app. Usage is counted on our own server, never per person.

sentiment_satisfiedKind by default

No streak shaming, no red alarms, no cheering for big deficits. It never suggests eating below a safe floor.

groupA household, not a feed

Share recipes, batches and pots with up to five people at home. No leaderboards, profiles or badges.

downloadYour data leaves with you

Export everything as CSV or JSON, or delete your account and all of it from inside the app.

infoNot a medical device

Hourgrain records and estimates. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything, and it's made for adults.

Questions

How long until my estimate is ready?

From day one you have a formula's estimate, clearly labelled. As you log and weigh in, your own data takes over, usually within two to three weeks, and the band shows how far along it is.

Do I have to weigh myself every day?

No. More weigh-ins make the trend steadier, but missed days are fine, and no single weigh-in moves your budget. Hourgrain never leads with today's number.

What if I don't log everything?

A steady gap cancels out, because the estimate is built from the same logs. Mark a day as partial when you know it is, and the engine leaves it out rather than guessing.

Can I bring my history from another app?

A MacroFactor export imports your food totals and weigh-ins, so your estimate can start settled. Cronometer and MyFitnessPal imports follow at launch, and you can export everything from Hourgrain any time.
